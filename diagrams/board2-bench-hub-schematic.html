Board 2 — bench hub, electrical schematic

Perfboard build. Solid lines are copper nets; a filled dot ● is a connection, wires that merely cross (no dot) are not connected. TB-CAR is a generic patch panel — K1/K2's A/B/RO terminals have no fixed destination; dashed lines below them show one example patching (Example 2, the default ECU+10AS session), not a permanent wire. Pin numbers for P2 are standard J1962 (OBD2) pin numbers; pin numbers for the ECU/10AS destinations are the connector pins documented in docs/gems-ecu-pinout.md and docs/10as-pinout.md.

This board is multi-use, not fixed-harness. K1-A/B/RO and K2-A/B/RO are generic — each session you patch them to whichever module you're working with (engine K-line, 10AS K-line, ABS, SRS, or the immobiliser line directly). The dashed wiring shown below is one example configuration (the default ECU+10AS session); see docs/board2-bench-hub.md “Example configurations” for others, e.g. patching K2-A straight to the immobiliser/mobilise line instead of the 10AS K-line.

Power is paired per channel — 12V-1/12Vi-1 go with whatever's patched onto K1, 12V-2/12Vi-2 with whatever's patched onto K2 — but the switched/ignition feed is one shared switch (SW2), gating 12Vi-1 and 12Vi-2 together, not two independent switches.

Signals with no J1962 pin (immobiliser/mobilise, MIL-shared) can't ride through P2 — they reach TB-CAR only as direct wires, shown separately below.
