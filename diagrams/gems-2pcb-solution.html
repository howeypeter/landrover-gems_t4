GEMS bench rig — 2-PCB solution

PCB1 (MAIN): Pico + L9637D smart adapter  ·  PCB2 (POWER): DC input, switching & ECU/adapter routing. Two boxes, joined by a 4-pin Molex. Pin-exact against the confirmed P38 GEMS bench pinout (C1017 K = pin 23, L = pin 20; C1033 pin 7 main, pin 8 ignition, grounds 5/9/10/16) and the ST L9637D datasheet (Doc ID 1765).

+12 V GND K-line L-line USB (power+data)

1 · Topology

Signal flow: laptop ⇄ USB ⇄ Pico ⇄ L9637D ⇄ K/L ⇄ PCB1 J1 ⇄ 4-pin ⇄ PCB2 J3 ⇄ (pass-through) ⇄ J4 8-pin ⇄ ECU. Power: adapter → PCB2 (protect → fuse → SW1) → both the ECU (pin 7 always, pin 8 via SW2) and PCB1's L9637D Vs.

K node = L9637D pin 6 + R1 (510 Ω to Vs) + C2 (1 nF to GND) + J1-C (to ECU K) + JP1 (to L). With JP1 closed the ECU K (p23) and L (p20) share this node — the configuration that unlocked the 0xDA manufacturer channel. Pop JP1 for a K-only run.

6 · Assembly & safety rules (do not skip)

Board-killers, in order of how often they bite:
  • L9637D pin-1 orientation. 12 V must land only on pin 7 (Vs). Pins 1–4 and 8 are the ≤7 V domain; a flipped SO-8 puts 12 V there and destroys U1 (and maybe the Pico). Verify the pin-1 notch before first power.
  • Vcc = 3.3 V from the Pico, never 5 V. U1 RX idles at Vcc; 5 V would be pushed into the non-5 V-tolerant GP1.
  • 12 V never reaches the Pico. The Pico is USB-powered from the laptop; the only 12 V consumer on PCB1 is U1 pin 7 (Vs). Do not tie J1-A to Pico VSYS.
  • Reverse polarity kills ECUs. D2 on PCB2's DC input is cheap insurance — a mis-polarised adapter is the classic bench ECU death.
Grounding: one common ground — adapter (−) = PCB2 GND bus = 4-pin J1/J3 pin B = PCB1 GND = L9637D pin 5 = Pico GND (also bonded to the laptop via USB) = ECU pins 5/9/10/16. The K-line only has a valid reference if the Pico/L9637D share ground with the ECU, so this bond is required, not optional.
Power sequence (bench): SW1 ON → ECU main (p7) + L9637D Vs live, Pico boots on USB → then SW2 ON = "ignition" (ECU p8), ECU enters run/diag state. First contact read-only (gems_t4 kline live --port COMx). The Vs TVS (D1) and in-car fusing matter only if this ever leaves the bench for a running vehicle.
Cabling: any cheap 4-pin / 8-pin connector with a 22 AWG harness is fine at bench currents (the ECU main draw is only a couple of amps with nothing actuating) — Molex, JST-XH, KF2510 or Dupont all work; just keep the +12 V / ECU- power contacts good for a few amps. Keep the 4-pin K/L cable short; the K/L pair riding alongside 12 V is fine at 10.4 kbit/s.
