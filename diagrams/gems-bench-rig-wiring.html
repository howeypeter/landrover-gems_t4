No car, no OBD socket. A PC power supply feeds 12 V to both the ECU and the adapter; the Pico + L9637D adapter wires straight to the GEMS ECU's connector. The goal is one thing: prove the adapter can talk to a real ECU on the bench.

03 Power & the ECU

The PSU wires are standard PC colors. The ECU pins are the part to confirm.

PC PSU wires

  • Yellow+12 V. Feeds L9637D Vs (via fuse) + ECU battery + ECU ignition.
  • BlackGround / 0 V. The common ground for everything. You have one — this is it.
  • GreenPS_ON. Jumper it to any black wire or the PSU stays in standby and puts out no 12 V.
  • Red / orange+5 V / +3.3 V — not used here. Some PSUs need a small load on 5 V to stay on.

GEMS ECU — the pins

  • pin 7Main +12 V — C1033, main-relay supply; this runs the ECU
  • pin 8Ignition +12 V — C1033, "ON" sense; wakes it
  • 5·9·10·16Ground — C1033, all four to PSU black
  • pin 23 *K-line — C1017 → L9637D pin 6. * NAS/US = 23, non-NAS = 20

18-way black plug (C1033) = power + ground; 36-way red (C1017) = K-line. Sensors, coils, injectors not needed for a comms test. There's no separate always-hot pin — pin 7 runs it.

04 Before you power on

Pins are from Land Rover service data — meter-check them, follow three rules, and one reassurance.

⚠

Meter-check the ECU pins before the yellow wire goes live

The pins above come from Land Rover's GEMS service data — cross-checked against two independent factory-derived pinouts, with the coil / MIL / fuel-pump pins validating exactly. But those tables are Discovery / Morgan GEMS, and 12 V on the wrong pin kills an ECU. So on your ECU, before power: ohm C1033 pins 5/9/10/16 to the ECU case (≈0 Ω confirms grounds), and confirm the K-line pin reads continuous to OBD socket pin 7.

NAS vs non-NAS picks the K-line pin: a US-market (NAS) car = C1017 pin 23; a UK/Euro car = pin 20. Wire the transceiver to the wrong one and it won't talk.

i

Black is your ground — and everything must share it

The PSU's black wire is 0 V ground. Without a common ground the K-line has no reference and nothing talks. Tie together: PSU black, L9637D pin 5, the Pico's GND (it comes up through USB from the laptop), and the ECU's ground pins.

i

Turn the PSU on, and fuse the +12 V

An ATX supply is dead until you jumper the green wire to a black (that's the on switch). Put a 1–2 A fuse in the yellow +12 V line before it splits to the ECU and adapter.

✓

The immobiliser won't block you here

You have no BeCM on the bench, but that's fine: the immobiliser only stops the engine from running — it doesn't block diagnostic comms. With power + ignition + K-line, the ECU should still answer an init and let you read its ID and fault codes. That's exactly the test: gems_t4 live --port COM5, read-only.
