Notes (Sheet 1):
  • 12 V lands ONLY on U1 pin 7 (Vs) via F1. It never reaches the Pico (USB-powered). Pins 1–4/8 are the ≤7 V domain.
  • U1 Vcc = +3V3 from the Pico (pin 36), never 5 V. C1 (100 nF) decouples Vcc→GND at the chip.
  • K node = U1.6 + R1 + C2 + JP1.2 + J1.3. R1 510 Ω pulls K up to Vs (required). C2 1 nF K→GND (≤1.3 nF).
  • JP1 fitted closed ties L (J1.4) to the K node — the 0xDA-channel enable.
  • U1 pin 2 (LO) = no-connect; pin 8 (LI) = GND. Verify L9637D pin-1 orientation before power.

Notes (Sheet 2):
  • Power path: J2(+) → D2 (reverse-polarity) → F2 (3–5 A) → SW1 → V12S.
  • V12S feeds J3-1 (to PCB1 / L9637D Vs) and J4-3 (ECU main, pin 7); SW2 (IGN) gates V12S → J4-4 (ECU ignition, pin 8).
  • K and L pass straight through: J3-3↔J4-2 (K, ECU p23), J3-4↔J4-1 (L, ECU p20).
  • GND is common: J2−, J3-2 and J4-5…8 (ECU p5/9/10/16) — one ground with PCB1 and the adapter.
  • D3 (TVS) and LED1/R2 are DNP/optional for the bench.
