SW24-pin screw terminal (12V_IN, 12V_OUT, 12V_OUT_LED, GND_LED) wired to an off-board illuminated switch — shared ignition switch for all J4 12V_IGN pins (ECU ignition feed, 10AS IGN). 12V_IN from V12S; 12V_OUT = 12V_IGN rail; 12V_OUT_LED tied to 12V_OUT; GND_LED to GND.—
D31.5KE24A — 1500 W / 24 V unidirectional TVS, DO-27 axial through-hole (part on hand). Across the supply between F2 and SW1 (net VF), so it clamps even with SW1 off: band (cathode) to VF, other end to GND. Clamps supply spikes; fitted. Leads ~1.0 mm → 1.3 mm holes (lead + 0.3 mm clearance). Standard perfboard holes are ~1 mm, so drill the two holes out to 1.3 mm or solder the leads flat onto the pads.—
J316-position, 3.81mm pitch — Amphenol Anytek OQ1632510000G (DigiKey 609-3829-ND), a male, shrouded, right-angle (90°) PCB header. The pigtail from Board 1's male OBD2 plug lands on a mating Amphenol Anytek TJ1631530000G pluggable terminal block (female, screw termination, 10A/300V), which plugs into this header — confirmed as a real mated pair, not placeholders. Pin 16 = V12S (12V out, Board 2 → Board 1); pin 5 = GND. Every pin has a fixed, 1:1 trace to the matching pin on J7 — J3 itself is never touched when reconfiguring a session.609-3829-ND
J716-position Phoenix Contact SPTAF push-in, 45°(135°) angled, 3.5mm pitch — same EL series/family as the 1862042 lead that started this: 2× SPTAF 1/6-3,5-EL (1862084) + 1× SPTAF 1/4-3,5-EL (1862068) ganged side by side (6+6+4=16), same 16A/160V/1.5mm² rating throughout. 16 positions at 3.5mm pitch runs ~55–60mm, fitting comfortably on the board's 85mm long edge with margin to spare. Wired 1:1 to J3 by fixed trace. This is the junction that was missing — it's the side you actually touch: any of its 16 terminals can be patched to a J6 position via a short jumper wire, push-in (no tool, no screw) for the current session, without disturbing J3's robust pigtail connection. Verify from the datasheet that adjacent housings maintain continuous 3.5mm pitch when ganged end-to-end before ordering.—
J6Patch landing — 2× Phoenix Contact SPTAF 1/4-3,5-EL (1862068), 45°(135°) push-in, 3.5mm, 16A/160V (same part as J7's 4-position block), laid out as 2 rows × 4 columns with mirrored rows (row a faces one way, row b the other, so each row's wires enter from its own side; if there is room, a gap of ~10–15mm between rows is the alternative — verify the 45° wire-entry geometry on the datasheet). Columns = nets: NET1…NET4; each column's a/b pair is tied (same copper net) and sits directly above/below its partner so they read as pairs. Nets are neutral on Board 2 (no IC): NET1 = Board 1 LINE1_U2 R/W, NET2 = LINE2_U2 RO, NET3 = LINE3_U3 R/W, NET4 = LINE4_U3 RO; the RO nets can be swapped freely. J7 patch wires land here; each net has a fixed trace to its two J4 headers.—
J4Output: 2 rows × 4 columns of keyed 2×2 headers (one per J6 a/b position; row a on top, row b mirrored below, nets in the middle channel) — Molex Micro-Fit 3.0 43045-0412: 3.00 mm pitch, 4-circuit dual-row, vertical through-hole (1.02 mm drills; verify suffix / mounting pegs vs the Molex drawing — 0415 is SMD, 0418 has pegs). Cables exit upward. Each header carries 12V_BAT (V12S), 12V_IGN (SW2), GND + its net pin (NETxa/NETxb); the two headers of a net share one copper net. Keyed = one orientation; cannot be confused with J3's OBD2 plug. Cables: ECU = 3 headers, 10AS = 2 headers (harness doubles GND wires for the ECU's 4 grounds). Any cable fits any header; a mis-plug only moves the data line (a↔b swaps harmless — tied). The 1×8 right-angle 43045-0400 option was dropped: 8 in a row need ~89 mm, more than the 85 mm edge.—
Board 2 is a dumb relay (no IC). Power: J2 → D2 (reverse-polarity) → F2 → SW1 → V12S, which feeds J3 pin 16 (to Board 1) and the 12V_BAT pin of every J4 header; SW2 switches the 12V_IGN pin of every J4 header; GND is bussed to every header. Signals: every J3 pin has a fixed 1:1 trace to J7. The only thing you rewire is a short push-in patch wire from a J7 terminal to a J6 position (2 mirrored rows × NET1…NET4; the a/b pair in each column is tied). A fixed trace carries each net to its two J4 headers (2 rows × 4 columns); per-device cables (ECU, 10AS, …) plug into J4 and the board never changes. Worked example (dashed = the one movable link): J3 pin 7 → (fixed) → J7 pin 7 → (patch wire) → J6 NET1a → (fixed, tied) → J4 NET1a/NET1b headers → device cable. Immobiliser 3-way: ECU + 10AS cables on the two NET3 headers, Board 1's LINE3 R/W and LINE4 RO wires landing on J6 NET3a/NET3b — all one net; patching only the RO wire = listen-only.
