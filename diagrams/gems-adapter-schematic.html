Design notes.
  • K-line only. The L9637D also has an L-line (LI/LO); GEMS/T4 doesn't use it, so LI is tied to GND and LO is left open (both give a defined state per the datasheet).
  • No external TX/RX pull-ups. The chip integrates them to VCC. Because VCC = 3.3 V, the RX output idles at 3.3 V — inside the Pico's non-5 V-tolerant GPIO range. Do not raise VCC to 5 V unless RX is level-shifted.
  • The 510 Ω on pin 6 is not a conflict. Pin 6 (K) is open-drain and carries both the pull-up (R1, up to VS/+12 V) and the K-line wire. That is the correct ISO 9141 node: the bus idles high through R1 and any device pulls it to 0 V to signal.
  • Fit D1 before going on-car. A running vehicle produces load-dump/transient spikes a clean bench PSU never does; the TVS keeps VS under the 40 V limit.
  • Read vs write is software. The hardware is identical either way; the wireless/read-only write gate lives in KwpClient, not in this circuit.
